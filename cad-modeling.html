Dodge Charger

Personal SolidWorks project recreating the third-generation Dodge Charger from reference photos — full exterior surfacing, headlight and grille detailing, and a rendered studio pass to test lighting and materials.

Fossil Watch

A reverse-engineering exercise: modeled a Fossil chronograph from reference photos, part by part, down to the exploded assembly of the case, dial layers, and strap, then worked through a full rendering pass to match the original's materials and lighting.
