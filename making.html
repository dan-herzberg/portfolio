Early-COVID Facemasks

During the early COVID-19 shortage, designed and printed reusable respirators on a home 3D printer, iterating across two versions — the first (orange) with a swappable ABS filter cap, the second (teal) using a cut vacuum-bag filter and foam block with a rubber-band and zip-tie strap system.
